# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the brief: static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, publishable on Vercel. No framework unless a concrete reason appears. Product data stays separate from presentation.

## Users

People in Brazil choosing a smartphone, an Apple product, or an accessory, and people who already own a device and need it evaluated or repaired. They arrive from search, social, or a shared link and convert by starting a WhatsApp conversation.

## Product Purpose

A commercial site for GusPhone: a store that sells smartphones, Apple products and accessories, and also runs a technical-assistance bench. Success is a visitor who finds a model or a service and starts a real consultation on the company's official WhatsApp.

## Positioning

GusPhone is a phone shop with its own repair bench. The site has to sell devices and, in a visually distinct section, offer technical care — diagnosis, screen repair, battery replacement, charging problems — without promising speed, original parts, or manufacturer authorization.

## Brand

- Name: GusPhone.
- Confirmed palette: charcoal #111318, graphite #20242C, silver #C7CBD1, ice white #F5F6F8, electric blue #2678F3, mid gray #777E89.
- Confirmed type: Inter for text, prices, navigation and specs; Sora (or a similar geometric face) for titles.
- The logo is a supplied image file, never recreated in type.
- Personality: dark, technological, metallic, refined for a commercial site. Blue is the interaction color only.

## Constraints

- Never invent prices, stock, ratings, warranties, discounts, free shipping, certifications, sales figures, testimonials, addresses, hours, social profiles, or a phone number.
- Where real data is missing, use clearly labeled demonstrative content.
- WhatsApp is the conversion channel. The official number is unconfirmed; every WhatsApp control must read it from one config value and stay inert until that value is a real number.
- Buttons must do something real: filter, search, scroll to a section, or open a consultation. No decorative controls.
- Do not claim a brand or category is in stock.

## Voice

Direct, commercial, in Brazilian Portuguese. Objective titles, no hype, no generic promotional slogans beyond the copy the brief supplies.

## Open decisions

- Official WhatsApp number.
- Confirmed address and opening hours.
- Official social profiles.
- Real catalog: models actually sold, storage, condition (new or used), prices, warranties.
- Which repair services the bench actually offers beyond the ones named in the brief.
- Logo file: the project folder contained only `images (1).jpg`, which could not be read in this environment. The logo slot waits for the original file.
